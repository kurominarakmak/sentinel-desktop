## Reporting A Vulnerability

Until a contact is published, do not disclose vulnerabilities or secrets in public issues, pull requests, logs, or discussions. Include a clear description, impact, reproduction steps, affected version or commit, and any proof of concept that can be shared safely.

## Handling

Reports will be acknowledged, assessed, and handled privately. The project will coordinate a fix and disclosure when practical. This is a good-faith safe-harbor policy for security research conducted without accessing data unnecessarily, disrupting systems, or violating applicable law.
